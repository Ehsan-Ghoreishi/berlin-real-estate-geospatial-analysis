Policy shocks and interest rates

A listing-count-weighted citywide index, overlaid with the ECB rate and seven real policy and macro events.

Rent freeze released

Average Kaltmiete rose +10.5% in the six months after the Constitutional Court struck down the Mietendeckel (April 2021).

Rate-hike trough

Sales prices bottomed at €5,145/m² in August 2023, as the ECB rate approached its 4.50% peak, then recovered once cuts began.

Price model

Predicting secondary-market sale price from structure, building quality, location and macro features. Each model is evaluated three ways, including an out-of-time test on 2025–26 listings it never saw.
